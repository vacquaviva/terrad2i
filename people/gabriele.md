Gabriele Accarino is a Postdoctoral Research Scientist at Columbia University and the LEAP NSF Science and Technology Center, working at the intersection of machine learning and Earth system science. His research focuses on multi-scale representations of geophysical processes (primarily based on wavelet transform), evaluation metrics for Earth system models, and multi-scale generative modeling for physics emulation. Related interests include data-driven weather and climate emulators, generative climate downscaling, and extreme weather event detection. He holds a Ph.D. in Environmental Sciences, a M.Sc. in Computer Engineering, and a B.Sc. in Information Engineering from the University of Salento, Italy. Before joining Columbia, he was Junior Scientist and Head of the Machine Learning Research Unit at the Euro-Mediterranean Center on Climate Change (CMCC), where he led projects on climate AI, digital twins, and environmental forecasting. He has also served as an adjunct professor teaching High Performance Computing & Machine Learning, and Information Processing Systems.
