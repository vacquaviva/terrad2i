Davide is a PhD student in Engineering of Complex Systems at the University of Salento in collaboration with CMCC Foundation, in Italy. He studies data-driven techniques for the coupling between Atmosphere and Ocean systems, with the goal of emulating the Earth System key variables at a Subseasonal-to-Seasonal scale.

Davide has both a strong theoretical Machine Learning background in distributed environments and strong Python and programming skills. His main interest is studying and designing data-driven approaches applied to climate change-related tasks in order to help research development in this field.

He is proficient in in Machine Learning algorithms, including cutting edge architectures like Transformers, Mamba, (Spherical) Fourier Neural Operators, UNet, etc.
